## 3. Full Description (English)

Looking for a fast, simple, and private way to calculate your monthly salary in Bangladesh?

**BD Salary Calculator** by ViveScript Solutions LLC is a lightweight, on-device payroll calculator designed specifically for employees, job seekers, HR professionals, and office workers across Bangladesh.

Understand your true earnings at a glance—from Basic Salary and essential allowances to Provident Fund deductions and actual take-home pay.

### 🌟 Why Choose BD Salary Calculator?

* **⚡ Instant Calculation (One-Screen UX):** Simply enter your basic salary, and your gross earnings, deductions, and take-home pay calculate instantly in real-time. No complicated menus or endless navigation.
* **🇧🇩 Tailored for Bangladesh:** Built specifically around Bangladesh workplace payroll structures, supporting BDT (৳) currency and South Asian number formatting (Lakhs & Crores: e.g., ৳১,২৫,০০০ and ৳1,25,000).
* **🌐 Bilingual Support (English & বাংলা):** Switch seamlessly between authentic Bengali and clean English with a single tap in the app header.
* **🔒 100% Private & Offline:** Your financial information is your business. All calculations happen entirely on your phone. No logins, no accounts, and zero cloud uploads of your salary figures.
* **💼 Comprehensive Breakdown:**
  * **Basic Salary (মূল বেতন):** Quick input with immediate calculations.
  * **Allowances (ভাতা সমূহ):** Housing allowance, medical allowance, transport allowance, other allowances, and custom allowance items.
  * **Deductions (কর্তন সমূহ):** Provident Fund (PF), estimated Income Tax deduction, other deductions, and custom deduction items.
  * **Net Take-Home Pay (হাতে পাওয়া বেতন):** Crystal-clear hero display showing your net earnings and percentage of gross pay.
  * **Annual Projections:** Automatic 12-month annual gross and annual take-home salary summaries.
* **📲 Share in Seconds:** One-tap copy and share lets you send a formatted salary breakdown to your clipboard, messaging apps, or email.

### 👥 Who Is This App For?

* **Job Seekers:** Evaluate new job offers and understand your real in-hand salary before signing an employment contract.
* **Employees & Professionals:** Verify monthly pay slips, check allowance distributions, and plan household budgets with confidence.
* **HR, Accounts & Admin Staff:** Perform quick on-the-spot salary estimations for candidate negotiations or employee queries.

### 🛡️ Privacy & Reliability

BD Salary Calculator works completely offline without requiring personal information, contacts, storage permissions, or user accounts.

Download **BD Salary Calculator** today and take the guesswork out of your monthly earnings!
