## 1. Introduction

ViveScript Solutions LLC ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use the **BD Salary Calculator** Android application.

Our core design principle is **privacy by design**: all salary calculations, allowances, deductions, and financial figures remain strictly on your device.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

* **No Personal Information:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, national IDs, or physical addresses.
* **No Salary or Financial Data Transmission:** All salary entries (basic pay, housing, medical, transport, provident fund, tax, take-home pay) are processed exclusively on your device. They are **never** transmitted to our servers or any external third party.
* **No Account Creation:** The application does not require account creation, registration, or social sign-in.
* **No Sensitive Permissions:** We do not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos/media storage, precise location, or SMS.

---

## 3. On-Device Local Storage

The application uses standard Android on-device storage (`SharedPreferences`) to optionally retain your last-entered salary inputs. This allows you to resume your calculation conveniently when reopening the application.
* This data is stored locally in the private app sandbox.
* It is never backed up to our servers.
* You can clear all stored values at any time using the in-app **Reset** button or by clearing app data in Android Settings.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

The application integrates with the **Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob)** provided by Google LLC to display non-intrusive advertisements.

Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain technical data to serve ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance, including:
* IP address (used for general geographic region/country-level ad serving)
* Pseudonymous device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / AAID)
* Diagnostic information, app performance metrics, and ad interaction logs

For more details on how Google processes ad data and how you can opt out of personalized advertising on Android, please consult:
* [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
* You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time via:  
  **Android Settings > Google > Ads > Delete/Reset advertising ID**.

---

## 5. Network Communication

Network communication is utilized solely by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to request and display advertisements. No personal financial calculations or user-authored contents are transmitted over the network.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

BD Salary Calculator is a general utility intended for employees, job seekers, and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions).

---

## 7. Security

Because all salary calculations are performed locally in-memory and in Android's private app sandbox, your financial figures are safeguarded by Android's application sandboxing architecture.
